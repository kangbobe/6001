A — Data, quality and preprocessing¶

tl;dr¶

This notebook recomputes training-only data checks and exploratory plots from the locked UCI legacy CSV. The training partition contains 27,126 records with 3,173 positive outcomes (11.697%). The research target is a retrospective simulation of pre-campaign customer screening. It is not a causal marketing-effect estimate. Exact pdays is replaced by prior-contact status; current-campaign fields are excluded. See the computed tables below for observed values.

Context & Methods¶

Source: UCI Bank Marketing, legacy bank-full.csv; CC BY 4.0. Frozen train / validation / test sizes: 27,126 / 9,042 / 9,043. All target relationships below use train only.

Key Assumptions¶

Records have no customer identifier or verified decision-time snapshots. Random splits assess historical record-level generalization. unknown is a category, negative balances are valid observations, and matching profiles are not proof of duplicate customers.

Takeaways¶

The exact training counts and category summaries above are descriptive associations, not effects of marketing or customer identity evidence. Use the shared preprocessing constructor inside each CV fit; the illustrative fitted transformer here is never fed into model tuning. No test prediction or test target-association analysis runs in this notebook. Full CSV tables and report-ready figures are under results/eda. 中文交接：字段可得性、数据来源、保留异常值的原因，以及划分限制应保留在报告中。
